    🛠️ Troubleshooting

    Nothing happens when I drag a window. First, confirm that Wobbly is actually running. Open the app again from your Applications folder. If it is already running, the system may bring it to the front rather than starting a second copy. Then try dragging a window that was already open before you started the app.

    The download did not start. Return to the download page using the button above and look for the most recent release. Sometimes browsers block downloads until you confirm them; check for a small banner or icon in your browser window asking for permission.

    My computer says the app cannot be opened. This is a standard safety message for software downloaded outside the official app store. Open your System Settings, find the privacy and security section, and look for a message about the app you just tried to open. Choose to allow it, then open the app again.

    The animation looks choppy. Close other applications that use a lot of graphics or memory, such as video editors or many browser tabs. Plugging in a power adapter can also help, since some computers slow down animations when running on battery.

    I want to remove Wobbly completely. Close the app, then drag it from your Applications folder to the Trash. Empty the Trash and the app is gone. No leftover settings remain on your system.
